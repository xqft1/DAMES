# VII. THE DAMES / BEERS V4 HOOK

APPROVED HOOK: https://github.com/xqft1/BEERS-DAMES-V4

The DAMES/BEERS market goes beyond simple meme lore.

A dedicated **Uniswap V4 hook** was created for the pair.

The design adds a burn mechanic directly to trading activity.

## Core idea

On qualifying DAMES/BEERS swaps, the hook is designed so that:

```text
1% of DAMES involved in the trade → burn
1% of BEERS involved in the trade → burn
```

The intention is to make market activity destructive to token supply rather than purely extractive.

Every qualifying trade becomes part exchange and part bonfire.

In folklore:

> Every round poured into the tavern casts a little coin into the hearth.

In protocol language:

> Trading activity can trigger programmed token burns through the V4 hook logic.

# IX. WHY A V4 HOOK?

Uniswap V4 introduces programmable hooks around pool actions.

A hook can execute custom logic at defined points in a pool's lifecycle.

For DAMES/BEERS, that programmability is used to give the trading pair behaviour beyond a conventional passive AMM pool.

The hook makes the pair itself part of the meme.

The burn is not merely something discussed on social media.

It is intended to be part of the market's on-chain mechanics.

That distinction is important.

```text
Normal meme:
"we should burn tokens"

BEERS:
"put the fire beside the market"
```
